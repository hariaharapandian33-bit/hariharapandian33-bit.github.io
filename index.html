SMART CARDIO-TWIN

AI-Powered Digital Health Monitoring & Early Pattern Detection

What is this?

Smart Cardio-Twin combines medical history + current readings into a Personal Digital Health Twin. It compares readings with your personal baseline and alerts for unusual patterns.

How it Works

1. Upload Reports / Enter Readings
2. AI Checks Pattern vs Baseline
3. Dashboard Shows Trends
4. Caregiver Alert if Enabled

For Whom?

Older adults, persons with disabilities, people who need regular cardiac monitoring.

Note: Supports awareness, not a diagnostic device.
